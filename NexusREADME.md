[center][color=#1e84cc][font=Verdana][size=6][b]Mojave Shimmer[/b][/size][/font][/color][/center]
[center][font=Verdana][b][i]You ever look into the distance, and think there's something... missing?[/i][/b] [/font][/center]

[font=Verdana]This mod aims to add a [url=https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mirage#Heat_haze]Heat Haze[/url]-like effect into New Vegas. Honestly it's a bare-bones implementation, but an implementation nonetheless (the effect isn't scientifically correct in the slightest)[/font]
[font=Verdana]This took HEAVY inspiration from [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/newvegas/mods/76286]doodlum's Heat Haze[/url] mod, and has been expanded with new meshes, textures, configuration options, and logic to match more realistic depictions of the effect.[/font]

[center][color=#1e84cc][font=Verdana][size=5][b]Why?[/b][/size][/font][/color][/center]
[font=Verdana]The main goal of this was following the ESP-less trend of converting a mod; since I'm all for FOSS.[/font]
[font=Verdana]Plus i wanted to try my hand at New Vegas-specific modding for the first time that wasn't just "resolve X to work with Y" in FNVEdit.[/font]

[center][color=#1e84cc][font=Verdana][size=5][b]Features[/b][/size][/font][/color][/center]
[list]
[*][color=#1e84cc][font=Verdana][b]ESP-less[/b] [/font][/color][font=Verdana]implementation of a distance Heat Shimmer effect.[/font][/*]
[*][font=Verdana]Custom (albeit simple) mesh for displaying the haze effect (literally just a cylinder with custom textures).[/font][/*]
[*][font=Verdana]Configurable through [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/newvegas/mods/93642]MCM Extender[/url] (optional) or the INI:[/font]
[list]
[*][font=Verdana][b][color=#1e84cc]Distance[/color][/b] (50-100m distances)[/font][/*]
[*][font=Verdana][b][color=#1e84cc]Start / end times[/color][/b] and the [b][color=#1e84cc]fade length[/color][/b] in / out[/font][/*]
[*][font=Verdana][b][color=#1e84cc]Fallout 3 areas[/color][/b] (Experimental, not fully tested)[/font][/*]
[/list][/*]
[*][font=Verdana][b][color=#1e84cc]Debug [/color][/b]options: Wireframe to show the distance, an [b][color=#1e84cc]Intensity Multiplier[/color][/b], and [b][color=#1e84cc]All Weathers[/color][/b] / [b][color=#1e84cc]All Times[/color][/b] to ignore the weather or time of day.[/font][/*]
[*][font=Verdana][i][b][color=#1e84cc]TTW Compatible [/color][/b][/i]for the most part[/font][/*]
[/list]

[center][color=#1e84cc][font=Verdana][size=5][b]TL;DR on how it works:[/b][/size][/font][/color][/center]
[list]
[*][font=Verdana]Draws a tall cylinder around the player[/font][/*]
[*][font=Verdana]Display the refraction texture effect on that cylinder[/font][/*]
[*][font=Verdana]Fades the effect at the start of the day, and out at the end.[/font][/*]
[/list]
